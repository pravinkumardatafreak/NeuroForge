  1. Open your live link on your phone.
  2. Tap the 📲 Install Mobile App button below (or tap the browser menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app).
🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open your live link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up ↑) at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
